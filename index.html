Your data syncs automatically to your private Firebase cloud database. Vendors, other expenses, and events are private to whoever added them unless explicitly shared — look for the "Share" toggle on each. Tasks are shared between everyone with access. Guest records are private to whoever added them unless explicitly shared, so each person sees their own guests plus guests shared with them.
